Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

OHANA Clean Solutions LLC ("OHANA", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use ohanacleansolutions.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version: we only ask for what we need to quote and schedule your cleaning, we use it only for that, and we never sell your information.

Information we collect

When you use our instant quote calculator or contact us, we may ask for:

  • Your name (first and last)
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • ZIP code, to confirm we serve your area

We also receive the details you choose in the calculator, such as the type of cleaning, home size, condition, add-ons, frequency, and your preferred date and time.

When you send your booking through WhatsApp, the message goes from your device through WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.

How we use your information

  • To give you a price estimate for your cleaning
  • To schedule and confirm your appointment
  • To contact you about your booking by WhatsApp, text message (SMS) or email
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support

We do not use your information for anything unrelated to your cleaning service.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their advertising. We only share it when needed to provide our service (for example, the messaging app you choose to contact us with) or when required by law.

Other services this website uses

To display the site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and map images from CARTO, and the site is hosted on Vercel. Like most websites, these providers may receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when the page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to provide and follow up on your service, or as required for our business records. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see below).

Deleting your information

To ask us to delete your information, email ohannacleann@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the name and phone number or email you used with us. We will confirm once it is done.

Your California privacy rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you and how we use it
  • Delete the personal information we have collected from you
  • Correct personal information that is inaccurate
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (we do not sell or share it)
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect it)
  • Not be discriminated against for using any of these rights

To use any of these rights, email ohannacleann@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on your request, and we will respond within the time required by California law.

Children

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
