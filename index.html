Questions?

How much is the deposit?
+
Our standard deposit is $50. It helps us secure your booking and is applied to your total after the service.
What's your cancellation policy?
+
You can cancel or reschedule up to 48 hours before your appointment for a full refund. Same-day cancellations are non-refundable.
Do you bring your own supplies?
+
Yes. We bring all professional products and equipment needed for a top-quality clean. If you prefer specific products for any reason, just let us know.
Do you clean inside appliances?
+
Yes, we offer add-ons for inside the fridge, inside the oven, and more. You can select these in the booking calculator above.
Do you service my area?
+
We serve 26+ cities across the Bay Area — San Jose, Palo Alto, San Francisco, Oakland, Fremont, Santa Clara, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, and more. Check the map above or enter your ZIP in the calculator to confirm your area.
