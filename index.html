How much is the deposit?
+
$50 secures your booking slot — applied toward your total. The remaining balance is paid on the day of service. No surprises, no hidden fees.

+
Yes. We bring all professional-grade products and equipment. If you have preferences or sensitivities, let us know before the appointment.
What's your cancellation policy?
+
Cancel or reschedule up to 48 hours before for a full refund. Same-day cancellations are non-refundable.
Is same-day service really available?
+
Yes — in most areas. Book in the morning and we can often clean the same afternoon. Availability confirmed instantly via WhatsApp at (669) 317-6911.
Do you do commercial and post-construction?
+
Absolutely. We clean offices, retail spaces, and handle post-construction clean-ups. These are quoted custom — contact us via phone or WhatsApp and we'll build a plan for your space.
What if I'm not happy with the result?
+
We return within 24 hours and make it right at no extra cost. Your satisfaction is guaranteed — no questions asked.
